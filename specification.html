Design Token

Philosophy & Principles

The design system exists to serve one principle above all: clarity before decoration. Every token, every component, every interaction is evaluated against whether it helps a reader understand more or distracts from that goal.

  • 01
    Beauty through clarity Elegance emerges from clear information hierarchy, generous whitespace, and purposeful typography — not ornamentation.
  • 02
    Calm as a feature The interface never shouts. Colors are natural and warm. Motion is intentional. The reader always knows where they are and what to do next.
  • 03
    Simple first, deep on demand Default states are clean. Complexity is revealed progressively as curiosity demands it.
  • 04
    Trustworthy by default Every interactive element communicates confidence. Hover states are subtle but precise. Every claim is traceable to its source.

Design Token

Motion

Animation is purposeful, never decorative. Every motion serves a functional purpose: orienting the user, showing a relationship, or providing confidence in an interaction.

TokenValueUsage
--dur-fast150msHovers, toggles, micro-interactions
--dur-normal250msPanel slides, card expansion, transitions
--dur-slow400msPage transitions, timeline zoom, graph layout
--ease-outcubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)Elements entering or appearing
--ease-inoutcubic-bezier(0.65, 0, 0.35, 1)Elements moving between states

Motion Principles

Fast by default

150ms for hovers and micro-interactions ensures the interface feels responsive, not sluggish.

Sliding, not fading

Elements that appear or dismiss slide from context (inspector from right, cards expanding upward). Fading alone disorients.

No parallax, no fluff

Scroll-driven animations, parallax effects, and decorative transitions are explicitly excluded.

Graph animations are smooth

The knowledge graph uses force-directed layout transitions at 400ms with ease-out for natural-feeling reorganization.

Voice & Tone

The platform's voice is that of a knowledgeable librarian — precise, helpful, and never condescending. It explains without oversimplifying and cites without lecturing.

AttributeApproachAvoid
ClarityPlain language first; technical terms link to glossariesJargon without explanation
CertaintyAttribution is always present: "Scholars suggest…" "The manuscript evidence indicates…"Absolute claims without evidence
ToneWarm, inviting, intellectually humblePreachy, dogmatic, or devotional language
ComplexitySimple first, deep on demand. Layers of detail available.Information dumping on first view
AI Voice"Based on the available sources…" — the AI is a guide, not an oraclePresenting AI output as unqualified fact

Microcopy Principles

  • Buttons — Use verbs: "Explore connections", "View sources", "Compare manuscripts"
  • Empty states — Never show a blank screen. "Start building your research workspace"
  • Errors — "Something went wrong loading this passage. The connection to our manuscript database was interrupted."
  • AI disclaimers — "AI responses are generated based on the sources in the knowledge graph. Always verify against primary evidence."
Specification

Vision

Build the world's most transparent, evidence-based platform for exploring biblical literature.

This is not a Bible application. It is a research and reading platform where users discover the biblical world through evidence rather than doctrine.

The platform does not answer theological questions on behalf of the user. It provides:

Primary texts Historical context Linguistic evidence Literary relationships Manuscript witnesses Ancient Near Eastern parallels Scholarly perspectives

Every conclusion traces back to its sources.

Specification

Product Philosophy

Guiding Principle

Simple First. Deep on Demand.

A first-time reader should never feel intimidated. A researcher should never feel limited. Complexity reveals itself only when curiosity demands it.

2
Primary sources before commentary

The text speaks first. Interpretation follows.

When evidence is ambiguous, we say so.

4

5

Operational Commitments

  • Every claim is traceable — Any assertion about the text links back to its supporting evidence.
  • AI explains; AI never invents — The AI assistant cites the knowledge graph and databases. It does not generate unsupported claims.

Design Inspirations

Notion

Clean editorial layout, generous whitespace, block-based information architecture, calm typography, minimal visual noise. Users feel free to explore without overwhelm.

Obsidian

Knowledge graph visualization, connected notes, relationship-first navigation, backlinks, bidirectional thinking. The Bible feels like an interconnected network, not isolated books.

Linear

Polished interactions, crisp typography, fast navigation, subtle animations, premium feel, keyboard-first productivity. The application feels responsive and deliberate.

Perplexity

Question-driven exploration, evidence-backed answers, source attribution, clean reading experience, AI assisting rather than replacing. Users feel they are researching, not chatting.

Google Maps — Primary Inspiration

Information reveals progressively with zoom depth. At first: roads. Then neighborhoods. Then buildings. Then businesses. The deeper the user explores, the richer the information becomes.

Specification

Design Language

The interface should feel like a modern research notebook, not a religious application.

What We Avoid

Parchment textures

No artificial aging, no papyrus backgrounds, no "ancient scroll" aesthetic.

Medieval fonts

No blackletter, no uncial, no faux-historical typefaces.

Gold ornaments

No decorative borders, no illuminated initials, no ornamental flourishes.

Excessive religious symbolism

No crosses, no halos, no stained-glass filters, no iconographic shortcuts.

Beauty comes from clarity.

Specification

Progressive Disclosure Framework

Every passage page operates across four layers, each unlocking richer exploration:

1

Read

Bible text, translation selector, reading controls, highlights, bookmarks, notes.

2

Understand

Historical background, timeline, people and places, short explanations, maps, cross-references. Plain language throughout.

3

Explore

Hebrew/Greek, lexicons, parallel passages, ancient literature, reception history, connections, interactive timelines.

4

Research

Textual criticism, scholarly debates, manuscript comparisons, source criticism, citation graph, academic references, variant readings.

Specification

Device Experience

Mobile — Reading Companion

Optimized for single-hand reading. Simplified navigation, swipe gestures, bottom-sheet inspector. Focus on layers 1 and 2 (Read, Understand).

Desktop — Research Studio

Multi-panel workspace. Full keyboard navigation. Sidebar, inspector, timeline, graph — all available simultaneously. All four layers accessible.

Both feel native to their device.

Specification

AI Philosophy

AI is never the source of truth.

The knowledge graph is. The databases are. The manuscripts are.

AI is an intelligent librarian that:

Explains

Complex concepts in plain language with source links.

Compares

Side-by-side analysis of manuscripts, translations, or interpretations.

Summarizes

Scholarly consensus and areas of debate.

Translates Complexity

Hebrew/Greek grammar, textual criticism, historical context.

Guides Exploration

Suggests connections, questions, and next steps.

Every AI response cites the evidence used to generate it.
